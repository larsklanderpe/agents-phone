Privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Agents Phone is an AI-assisted text messaging service operated by Lars Klander. It helps people communicate with Lars, arrange meetings, and handle related requests on his behalf.

This policy explains how we handle information when you choose to text the agent or visit its website. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Lars Klander, the operator of Agents Phone.

Information we handle

When you interact with Agents Phone, we process your mobile phone number, the messages you send and receive, and any information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, availability, time zone, or meeting details. We also process messaging records such as timestamps, delivery status, and your opt-in and opt-out requests.

Please provide only what is needed for your request. Do not send passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or sensitive medical information by text.

How we use information

We use this information to understand and respond to your requests, coordinate meetings and related follow-ups, maintain the context of your conversation, and give Lars the information needed to assist you. We also use messaging records to respect your communication preferences, troubleshoot delivery problems, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

You are communicating with an AI assistant acting on Lars's behalf. Messages and relevant conversation context are processed by AI services to generate responses. Lars may review conversations and take over when needed. You can ask to speak with Lars directly.

Text opt-in and your choices

You opt in by sending a text that explicitly agrees to receive messages from Agents Phone, using the number and instructions provided to you. Consent applies to conversations, scheduling, and related service messages from Agents Phone on Lars's behalf. It does not authorize unrelated promotional messages.

Message frequency varies with your requests and conversations. Message and data rates may apply. Text messaging is optional, and consent is not a condition of purchase.

The SMS program is also described in our Terms & Conditions, including SMS Terms.

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You may receive one confirmation of your opt-out, after which further messages will stop unless you opt in again. Reply HELP for assistance, or email lars.klander@gmail.com. You can also use that email to contact Lars without using the agent.

No sale or marketing sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share mobile phone numbers, message content, or other personal information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not sold, transferred, or shared for another party's messaging or marketing campaigns.

Running the service requires the operational processing described below. Relevant scheduling details may also be processed by calendar services to fulfill your meeting request. This processing supports your interaction with Lars; it does not give other parties permission to contact you for their own marketing.

We may disclose information if required by law or legal process. We do not otherwise share your conversations with unrelated people or businesses.

Messaging and AI providers

Twilio handles text messaging. It processes phone numbers, message content, and messaging records to receive and deliver texts and support messaging operations.

The current AI agent is Grok Bot. Your messages and relevant conversation context are processed through that agent's AI services to understand your requests and generate responses.

We plan to use separate phone numbers for other agent setups. Future setups may include Claude, Codex, Cursor/SpaceX, Gemini, or other AI systems. The AI services involved depend on the agent associated with the number you contact. Naming possible future systems here does not mean your messages are sent to all of them.

We will identify the agent used for a number in its opt-in information and update this policy as providers change. Your opt-in for one number does not enroll you in conversations with agents on other numbers. You can ask which agent handles a number by emailing lars.klander@gmail.com.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as needed to handle your requests, maintain relevant conversation and scheduling records, resolve problems, and meet legal obligations. We may retain a limited record of your opt-out so we can continue to honor it. Service providers may retain operational records under their own retention policies.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but text messaging is not an end-to-end encrypted channel and no service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be processed in countries where our providers operate.

Access, corrections, and deletion

To ask what information we hold about you, correct it, request deletion, or raise a privacy concern, email lars.klander@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law, including any required retention. Stopping messages does not automatically delete earlier conversations.

Website visits

The Agents Phone information pages do not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or cookies set by us. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's Pages documentation and its privacy statement. Other websites linked from these pages have their own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

We will post updates to this policy here and revise the effective date when our practices change. If a change requires new consent, we will request it before using information for that purpose.

Agents Phone is operated by Lars Klander. For privacy questions or help, contact lars.klander@gmail.com.
