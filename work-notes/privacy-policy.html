Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Work Notes is operated by Lars Klander under the registered SMS brand Lars Klander Personal. The program uses +1 (501) 464-4426 for private work requests and related updates.

This policy applies specifically to Work Notes. It does not enroll you in the separate Agents Phone program. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean Lars Klander.

Information we process

We process the sender's mobile phone number, message content, timestamps, delivery information, and work-request details. The enrollment form collects your name, email address, optional mobile number, and SMS consent choice, together with a submission timestamp and consent wording version. We also process approval status and opt-out records when applicable. Please send only information needed for your work request, and do not include passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Messages from approved numbers are forwarded to a private Slack channel for handling. Lars and people or assistants authorized to handle the request may review the information. Relevant details may be entered into work-management systems, such as a support ticket system, to fulfill the request.

Information is used to process work requests, maintain conversation context, provide related acknowledgments and updates when outbound messaging is enabled, troubleshoot delivery, prevent misuse, and honor messaging preferences. AI services may process message content to interpret requests or prepare responses. AI output can contain mistakes and should be checked before relying on it.

Service providers and access

Tally hosts the enrollment form and stores submitted answers and consent records, which Lars can access to review enrollment requests and messaging preferences. Providing a mobile number alone does not grant SMS consent. The separate consent checkbox must be selected.

The planned two-way messaging service will run on Render. It will keep a private copy of enrollment and consent records, approval and opt-out history, message content, delivery records, and the information needed to connect a Slack conversation to its sender. Lars will use these records to manage participation and verify delivery. This hosting arrangement does not make your messages or enrollment records public.

Twilio processes phone numbers, message content, and delivery records for SMS transport. Slack stores forwarded messages and makes them available to authorized members and integrations in the private work channel. Connected AI and work-management services process relevant information to carry out requests. Processing may occur outside your country.

For information about the assistants currently connected to Work Notes, contact lars.klander@gmail.com. We may disclose information where required by law. We do not make unrelated conversations publicly available.

No sale or marketing sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Operational processing by the service providers described above does not authorize those providers to use your consent for their own messaging campaigns.

SMS consent and choices

Receiving automated outbound Work Notes messages requires affirmative consent and approval for participation. Viewing these pages, submitting a work request, or being included on an approved-sender list does not by itself establish consent to recurring outbound messages.

Message frequency varies with your requests and their progress. Message and data rates may apply. SMS participation is optional and consent is not a condition of purchase. Work Notes does not send unrelated promotional messages.

Once outbound messaging is available, reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. You may receive one opt-out confirmation, after which program messages stop unless you consent again. You can also request help or withdrawal of consent by emailing lars.klander@gmail.com.

You can request enrollment online. The SMS consent checkbox is optional and unchecked by default. A mobile number is required only if you select SMS consent. You can submit without consenting to texts and communicate by email instead. Outbound program replies are not yet available; enrollment requests require review and approval before messages begin. See the SMS Terms.

Retention and security

We retain information as needed to process work requests, preserve relevant work records, resolve problems, and satisfy applicable obligations. A limited consent or suppression record may be retained to honor messaging preferences. Copies held by service providers are also subject to their retention arrangements.

We take reasonable steps to limit access. SMS is not end-to-end encrypted, and no service can guarantee absolute security.

Access, correction, and deletion

Contact lars.klander@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. Identity verification may be required. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and necessary retention of work or consent records. Unsubscribing does not automatically delete prior messages or tickets.

Tally's processing of technical information and use of cookies are described in Tally's Privacy Policy. Linked services have their own privacy practices.

Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. A policy update does not expand your SMS consent. Additional consent will be obtained where required.
