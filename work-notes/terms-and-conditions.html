About Work Notes

Approved participants text work requests to a private Slack channel, where Lars and authorized people or assistants can process them. The intended two-way service provides acknowledgments, clarification questions, ticket confirmations, progress reports, and completion updates related to those requests.

Online enrollment requests are available and subject to review by Lars. Outbound program replies are not yet available. Submitting an enrollment request does not immediately activate messages.

Work Notes is not an emergency service. Delivery, response times, and completion of requests are not guaranteed. AI-assisted responses can be incorrect; verify important details and contact Lars when clarification is needed.
